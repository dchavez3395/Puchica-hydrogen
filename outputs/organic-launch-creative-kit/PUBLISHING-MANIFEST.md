## Publishing checklist

Before each approved post:

1. Confirm the channel-specific UTM source.
2. Confirm any Canada-only audience restriction.
3. Upload without an auto-applied enhancement that changes product colour.
4. Add the supplied alt text where the platform supports it.
5. Do not add a discount, free-shipping promise, fixed delivery date, review
   count, sales count, or scarcity statement.
6. Record the publication date, channel, live URL, and baseline session count.

Publishing, scheduling, public posting, boosting, and ad spend all require the
owner’s explicit approval.
