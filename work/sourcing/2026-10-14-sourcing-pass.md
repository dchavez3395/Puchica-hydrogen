# Sourcing pass — 2026-10-14

Bar: **≥ CA$50 contribution per order** (paid CPA benchmark CA$42–48), **≤ 14 days to Canada**, 120 V.
Contribution = price − supplier cost − freight − 7% duty on cost − card fees (2.9% + 0.30) − 8% returns reserve.
FX assumed US$1 = CA$1.40. Costs are the DSers API ("stable purchase") price, not the AliExpress page/welcome-deal price.

## Why new AliExpress products don't fix it

- **Small items** clear free shipping but are the same factory goods amazon.ca sellers stock, so price lands at the
  same ~35% margin, ~CA$25–40 per unit, with 2-day Prime delivery against our 10–21 days.
- **Large items** carry Canadian retail prices but ship on the oversize line (US$100+ freight), which eats the margin.

## Recommendation

1. Merchandise pairs/trios on the PDP: quantity presets "1 · 2 · 3" with the room photo of a pair/trio,
   copy "hang two over a table, three over an island". Ads point at the pair/trio view, not the single lamp.
2. Lead ads with the five highest-contribution pendants (lantern, egg, hat nest, pear, globe).
3. Read success as orders ≥ 2 units; a single-lamp order from an ad is a loss and that is expected.
4. Not now: new AliExpress categories. Revisit only with a Canadian/US-warehouse supplier, where delivery speed
   (not price) is the edge.
