# Bundle-product screen — 2026-08-02

## Decision

No product in this pass qualifies as Puchica's paid-acquisition hero. The
four-piece linen organizer is the strongest conditional merchandising lead,
but its exact pack count is not reconciled to the cheapest DSers rows and its
normal retail price leaves limited absolute contribution for paid social.

The 25-piece drawer organizer is explicitly configured and has a viable U.S.
supplier card, but current public pricing is too low. The 12-box shoe-storage
set has a suitable retail price but its credible exact-configuration supplier
cost is too high. No product was imported, published, ordered, paid for, or
added to an advertising campaign.

## Conservative economics

### Four linen boxes

If the established US$13.39 row were proven to be the four-pack, U.S. landed
cost would be US$15.38. At US$33.49 retail, a 2.8% + $0.30 planning fee and 15%
return/problem reserve leave roughly US$11.85 contribution, or 35.4%, before
missing costs. The initial target CAC would be only about US$8.29. Canada is
tighter after currency conversion. This is not enough room to approve ads.

### Twenty-five drawer trays

Using the exact-card high item cost of US$13.25 and its displayed free U.S.
shipping, US$17.99 retail leaves roughly US$1.24 contribution, or 6.9%, after
the planning fee and reserve. This category is commercially incompatible with
paid acquisition through the observed supplier.

### Twelve shoe boxes

At US$56.99 retail and US$41.23 landed cost, the planning fee and 15% reserve
leave roughly US$5.32 contribution, or 9.3%, before missing costs. Lower visual
match ranges cannot be assigned to the 12-pack without exact option proof.

## Channel conclusion

Puchica should separate two decisions:

1. **Organic merchandising:** products with good dual-market routes and modest
   contribution may remain eligible for organic/no-spend content after the
   ordinary-sales disclosure gates pass.
2. **Paid acquisition:** do not advertise a commodity merely because it has a
   positive gross margin. Require an explicit configuration, defensible normal
   retail above roughly US$40, worse-market landed cost below US$16–18, and
   enough absolute contribution to support acquisition plus missing costs.

The next paid-product search should prioritize a single-supplier curated bundle
whose combined shipping can be quoted before import, or a differentiated
organizer unavailable at mass-retailer commodity pricing. Supplier history,
exact configuration and both-market route remain mandatory.
