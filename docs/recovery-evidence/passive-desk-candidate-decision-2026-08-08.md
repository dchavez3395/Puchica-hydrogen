Supplier Optimizer is an image-similarity tool that warns its own data may be
incorrect. It is useful for route comparison after an exact public supplier
item has been identified; it is not reliable enough to originate the launch
catalog by itself.

The next sourcing cycle must start with exact public item IDs and then reconcile
the same ID, option and route inside DSers. Stop after 12 candidates per launch
slot and reject immediately on missing route, option ambiguity, branded media,
or item-plus-shipping cost above the applicable ceiling.
