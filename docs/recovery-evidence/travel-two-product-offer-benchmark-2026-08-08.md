## Hard decision gates

### IMPLEMENT in DSers / commercial GO

Proceed with the two-product cohort only when all are true:

1. The exact packing-cube SKU has a current, tracked Canada route and **landed cost ≤ CA$14.90**. This supports CA$54.99 list with at least about CA$5.73 CAC room after the 30% floor.
2. The exact cable-case SKU remains at or below CA$8.55 landed with a tracked route.
3. Exact options, contents, dimensions, materials, stock and media match the storefront claims in both products.
4. The duo is listed at CA$54.99 (CA$46.74 after 15% promo), without stacking another storewide discount.
5. U.S. is not advertised until the same exact-SKU route test is completed for the U.S.

### HOLD

- Packing cubes land at **CA$14.91–17.50**: CA$54.99 no longer preserves CA$5 CAC room; CA$59.99 can, but requires a retail/presentation justification.
- Packing cubes land at **CA$17.51–22.51**: CA$59.99 only preserves the 30% floor with thin-to-zero CAC. Organic offer at best; not paid-ad ready.
- Any exact route, option, stock, tracking or image/content fact remains unresolved.

### STOP DSers / commercial NO-GO

- Packing cubes land above **CA$22.51**, or the route is untracked/too slow/unstable.
- The only workable bundle price is above CA$59.99 for ordinary unbranded products.
- The exact six-piece contents or double-layer case configuration cannot be documented.
- Any margin case relies on a first-order coupon, welcome deal, coins, clearance price or other non-repeatable supplier discount.

## Bottom line

The market supports the concept, but **does not support paying to acquire traffic while the packing-cube landed cost is unresolved**. Refresh that one exact route first. If it is ≤CA$14.90, implement the cohort at CA$54.99 and complete offer/media QA. If it is >CA$17.50, stop trying to force this duo into paid acquisition.
