# Next launch cohort sourcing brief — 2026-08-08

## Objective

Source a coherent **three-product core, with up to two supporting products**, for
one customer problem: keeping a suitcase and everyday travel essentials orderly.

The cohort must be passive, non-electrical, lightweight, easy to demonstrate,
and simple to describe accurately. Products below are archetypes to source, not
supplier approvals. No listing moves to Shopify approval until its exact variant
passes the operating quality gates.

## Cohort architecture

### 1. Compression packing-cube set — core acquisition product

- **Required form:** four-to-six-piece zipper-compression set with visibly
  distinct sizes; no vacuum valve or pump.
- **Must-have facts:** exact piece count; dimensions and weight for every piece;
  shell and mesh material; zipper count and compression method; colorfast/care
  instructions; exact included storage/laundry bags.
- **Preferred:** neutral color family, reinforced handles, double zipper,
  product weight at or below 750 g.
- **Target regular retail:** CA$39–55 / US$29–39.
- **Disqualify:** unclear piece count, welcome-deal-only economics, mismatched
  gallery sets, “waterproof” or compression-percentage claims without proof,
  visible third-party marks, garment or suitcase included in the offer, more
  than six customer-selectable variants at launch.

### 2. Hanging toiletry organizer — core product

- **Required form:** zippered toiletry case with a fixed or stowable hanging
  hook; no liquid containers included.
- **Must-have facts:** closed/open dimensions, product weight, material, hook
  material, pocket count, closure type, care, and exact included parts.
- **Preferred:** two sizes or fewer, wipe-clean lining, neutral colors, 250–500 g.
- **Target regular retail:** CA$29–45 / US$22–34.
- **Disqualify:** dry-bag/roll-top imagery mixed with zip-case copy, IP rating or
  submersion claim, “TSA approved,” cosmetic/liquid bundle, fragile mirror,
  branded logo without resale evidence, or generic color codes exposed to the
  customer.

### 3. Travel cable and small-electronics pouch — core product

- **Required form:** passive zip pouch with elastic loops and pockets; no
  charger, battery, cable, tracker, or electronic component included.
- **Must-have facts:** exterior/interior dimensions, weight, shell/lining
  material, pocket/loop count, closure and care; clear statement that pictured
  electronics are not included.
- **Preferred:** one size, two neutral colors, 150–350 g.
- **Target regular retail:** CA$24–36 / US$18–27.
- **Disqualify:** RFID, fireproof, waterproof, anti-static, impact-proof, or
  device-compatibility claims without test evidence; included electronics;
  supplier logos; ambiguous single/double-layer options.

### 4. Shoe and laundry separator bag set — supporting product

- **Required form:** two-to-four reusable zip or drawstring bags for separating
  shoes and laundry inside luggage.
- **Must-have facts:** exact quantity, dimensions, material, closure, weight,
  wash/care method, and intended maximum shoe size if claimed.
- **Preferred:** washable polyester, under 350 g per set, no rigid components.
- **Target regular retail:** CA$22–32 / US$16–24.
- **Disqualify:** antimicrobial, odor-proof, waterproof, or “sanitizing” claims;
  unclear quantity; shoe-size claim unsupported by dimensions; disposable
  material presented as durable.

### 5. Passport/document organizer — supporting product

- **Required form:** passive zip travel-document wallet for passports, cards,
  tickets, and receipts.
- **Must-have facts:** exterior dimensions, weight, material, pocket/card-slot
  count, closure, and what is not included.
- **Preferred:** unbranded, one size, two neutral colors, under 300 g.
- **Target regular retail:** CA$24–36 / US$18–27.
- **Disqualify:** RFID-blocking claim without a credible test report, theft-proof
  or security guarantee, tracker/electronics, copied passport/airline marks,
  or imagery containing readable personal data.

## Shared supplier and product screen

Use these as sourcing floors, not as proof of approval:

- Generic/unbranded product or documented authorization to resell the brand.
- Product rating preferably at least 4.7/5 with at least 100 completed orders;
  supplier positive-feedback rate preferably at least 95%. Record the actual
  numbers and date—never turn them into storefront review claims.
- Exact mapped supplier stock and Shopify inventory each at least 25 units per
  launch variant.
- At most six sellable variants for the initial launch; every displayed option
  must be mapped, in stock, accurately named, and independently profitable.
- No batteries, plugs, magnets, blades, liquids, food contact, load-bearing use,
  medical/hygiene function, child/pet safety dependency, counterfeit risk, or
  regulated performance claim.
- Product plus packaging should remain below 1 kg unless the destination quote
  proves a stronger economic case.
- Supplier media must show the exact selected configuration consistently. Reject
  galleries that combine different products, quantities, hardware, dimensions,
  colors, or logos.

## Economics gate

The price bands above are working targets, not predetermined selling prices.
For each exact variant and destination:

1. Use the supplier's ordinary item price—not a welcome deal, coin discount,
   flash sale, or coupon that a repeat order may not receive.
2. Calculate landed cost as item cost + supplier shipping + duties/brokerage +
   any non-recoverable tax or currency buffer borne by Puchica.
3. Model the lowest customer price created by every active discount.
4. Subtract payment fees and a documented refund/defect reserve.
5. Require conservative contribution margin of at least 30% of the customer's
   charged merchandise price for the lowest-margin sellable variant.

As an early screening shortcut only, target landed cost no higher than 45% of
the worst-case discounted merchandise charge. The full contribution calculation
still controls approval.

## DSers evidence packet — capture for every exact variant

Save screenshots or exports with date, destination, product ID, and variant ID.

### Identity and mapping

- Shopify product ID, variant ID, handle, customer-facing option name and SKU.
- DSers store name, mapped-product record, supplier listing URL/item ID,
  supplier store name/ID, and mapped supplier option/SKU.
- DSers mapping screen showing every Shopify option paired to one available
  supplier option; capture any primary or secondary supplier mapping separately.
- Source image for the exact option and a screenshot of the complete option list.
- Ship-from location and mapped inventory; record all DSers warning banners.

### Supplier quality

- Supplier feedback rate, store age where shown, product rating, review count,
  completed-order/sold count, and date observed.
- Recent one-, three-, and five-star review themes; do not copy reviews to the
  storefront or imply Puchica customer reviews.
- Supplier return/refund terms, processing time, tracking commitment, and any
  packaging/logo information.

### Cost and route — capture twice

Use one real Canadian postal-code context and one real US ZIP context without
placing an order. For each destination capture:

- exact selected option and quantity one;
- ordinary item price and currency;
- shipping method, shipping cost, tracked status, dispatch estimate and delivery
  window;
- tax shown, duty/brokerage treatment if stated, and final pre-payment total;
  do not use a first-order/welcome/coupon total;
- destination country and postal/ZIP context visible in the same evidence;
- timestamp and supplier stock at the time of the quote.

### Product truth

- Exact piece count, dimensions, packed weight, materials, closure/hardware,
  included parts, care and warnings from the supplier source.
- Country of origin and best-supported HS classification for cross-border setup.
- Original media URLs and source/usage-rights basis.
- Human comparison of every intended storefront image against the mapped
  variant for geometry, quantity, color, dimensions and included parts.

## Fail-closed decision record

Record each variant/destination as `approved`, `quarantined`, or `not checked`.
`Not checked` is externally equivalent to quarantined. A supplier SKU, DSers
mapping, product rating, Shopify Active state, attractive image, or apparent
item-cost margin cannot approve a product by itself.

The cohort proceeds to storefront work only when at least three products in this
brief have one or more exact variants approved for the same initial destination.
