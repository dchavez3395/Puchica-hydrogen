# Public supplier specification cross-check — 2026-08-08

## Purpose

This read-only cross-check tests whether public product evidence supports the
two remaining grooming candidates after the signed-in DSers route review. It
does not replace exact DSers option evidence and does not approve either item.

## Mini hair iron: critical voltage blocker

The mapped DSers supplier title is:

> A mini hair iron pink corrugated plate electric curling iron curl modelling tools

An indexed AliExpress listing mirror with that exact title records:

- power source: electric;
- fixed, non-rotatable power cord;
- voltage: 210–240V;
- plug options: US, EU, UK and AU;
- operating range: 180–220°C;
- product type: monofunctional straightener.

Source: [Alitools indexed supplier listing](https://alitools.io/en/showcase/a-mini-hair-iron-pink-corrugated-plate-electric-curling-iron-curl-modelling-tools-32808739415)

This evidence shows why `Pink-US` cannot be interpreted as proof of Canadian or
United States electrical compatibility. It may mean a US-shaped plug attached
to a 210–240V appliance. The indexed mirror may represent an older listing with
the same title rather than the exact currently mapped item, so it is a critical
blocker—not conclusive proof of the current SKU's voltage.

Decision: **block the mapped mini hair iron** until the exact `Pink-US` option
shows an input label that explicitly supports 120V/60Hz or 100–240V/50–60Hz.
Do not publish, order, rewrite or advertise it based on plug shape alone.

## Straightener brush: family evidence does not resolve SKU `03`

Public pages using the same supplier-title family describe rechargeable,
Type-C hair-straightening brushes, but they do not establish what DSers option
`03` contains. One retail mirror describes rapid heating, multi-level control,
Type-C charging and cordless operation without exposing an auditable model or
complete electrical specification. A different current AliExpress-family
listing is priced near CA$25 but is not proven to be the same mapped item.

Sources:

- [Same-title retail mirror](https://www.ayzeze.com/en/products/6936889cf2878101b3768a23)
- [Current AliExpress-family price archive](https://www.pricearchive.org/aliexpress.com/item/1005010410900744)

Decision: keep the brush **conditional**. Public family evidence is not enough
to map opaque option `03` to a colour, model, battery, temperature range,
charger or included-parts bundle. Exact DSers/supplier option media is still
required.

## Audit implication

There is currently no verified two-product grooming pair:

- the mini hair iron is blocked on voltage compatibility;
- the straightener brush remains conditional on exact option identity; and
- the milk frother remains a separate, economically fragile home/kitchen item.

This is not evidence that the entire current catalog must be discarded. It is
evidence that launch selection must continue through the next coherent product
tranche rather than forcing this first group through.
