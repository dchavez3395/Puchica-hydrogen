## Final mini-cluster decisions

Do not launch or rebuild around the current two products. The current mapped variants fail product truth/compatibility. Do not force the lamp and frother together. Individually, frother ID `1005009033050005` and lamp ID `1005011845601959` merit a future exact-option detail check, but neither is approved and they do not form one acquisition offer. The store should not use two salvageable products as an excuse to repeat the broad general-store catalog.
