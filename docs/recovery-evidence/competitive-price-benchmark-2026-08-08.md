**Decision use:** commercial triage only. This is not approval to reprice, publish, advertise, or buy.

## Method and guardrails

- Prices were accessed on 2026-08-08 from retailer pages, retailer category results, and one clearly labeled third-party price tracker. Retail prices can change at any time.
- The evidence file distinguishes `exact_product_family`, `close_comparable`, `category_comparable`, `value_comparable`, and `premium_comparable`. “Exact product family” does **not** mean the selected variant, accessories, plug, warranty, fulfillment route, or supplier mapping is identical.
- Bands are judgment ranges built from the observed evidence, not statistical market averages. Sale prices, marketplace outliers and premium brands were not allowed to set the band by themselves.
- No currency conversion was used to declare a US price gap. Puchica's captured catalog price is CAD; the live US-market price was not captured here.
- Taxes and shipping are excluded unless a source explicitly bundled them. Several marketplace offers showed shipping separately or only after destination selection.
- A competitor price never overrides landed-cost economics. If Puchica's minimum profitable price is above the plausible band, the right answer is to reject the product or source a better route—not to sell at a loss.

The row-level evidence, source URLs, currencies and matching notes are in [competitive-price-benchmark-2026-08-08.csv](./competitive-price-benchmark-2026-08-08.csv).

## Required next decision gate

For each product, calculate:

`minimum viable retail = landed product cost + payment fees + expected refund/defect allowance + support/operating allowance + required contribution margin`

Then apply this rule:

1. If minimum viable retail is inside the plausible band, continue supplier and compliance verification.
2. If it is above the band but a real premium difference can be evidenced, document that value and test it without unsupported claims.
3. If it is above the band and no premium difference exists, reject the product or source a better supplier.

On current evidence, the order of attention should be:

1. **Drill:** verify exact route and economics; pricing could work in Canada.
2. **Cordless straightener brush:** verify safety and landed economics; potentially recoverable.
3. **Mini 2-in-1:** require a much better cost/price position or reject.
4. **Boykeep camera:** exact-option mapping plus major price correction required.
5. **Essager charger:** exact mapping plus major price correction/re-source required.
6. **Frother:** reject or replace unless a dramatic cost-based reprice is possible.
