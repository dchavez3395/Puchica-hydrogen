## Method and limits

- Scope was frozen to the five current Shopify/DSers product pairs supplied by the project lead. No new products were sourced or imported and no mappings were changed.
- Supplier Optimizer was run by the current Shopify product image for Canada and the United States.
- At most the three AliExpress results with the highest visible DSers `Sale` count were retained per product.
- Exact item IDs came from DSers result row keys and were confirmed by the AliExpress item URL opened by DSers. Exact public titles came from the opened tab title. AliExpress body inspection was blocked by the browser safety layer, so option selectors, certificates, stock, and specifications not present in DSers were not inferred.
- DSers columns are reported exactly as `Sale`, `Reliability`, `Response`, `Delivery`, and `Rating`. These must not be relabelled as verified review counts or third-party test scores.
- Supplier-alternative stock was **not displayed** in Supplier Optimizer. Current-map stock below is the DSers product-card figure only.
- CA rough landed estimates use the session-implied DSers conversion of about **CA$1.395 per US$1** and the maximum displayed item price plus displayed CA shipping. They exclude tax, duty/brokerage, payment fees, refunds, replacements, discounts, ad spend, and support. They are screening figures, not contribution margin.
- The US storefront price was not inspected in this bounded task; US gross margin is therefore not calculated.

## Claims and safety gate

Until an exact SKU is mapped and documented, do not publish or advertise:

- voltage, wattage, charge time, runtime, battery capacity, universal charging, or certification;
- exact temperature, fast-heating time, thermal protection, “safe for all hair,” damage prevention, or negative-ion outcomes;
- waterproof/IP rating unless the exact selected SKU has no-cost supplier or listing documentation that substantiates the rating;
- pressure range, gum-health, plaque-removal, whitening, braces safety, dental/professional claims, sterilization, sanitization, or oral-contact material safety;
- blade material, RPM, zero-gap precision, quietness, waterproofing, body/pet use, self-sharpening, professional/barber approval, or Canadian warranty;
- any accessory count or “what’s included” claim not tied to the selected option;
- a DSers `Sale` count or DSers service metrics as customer review proof on Puchica.
