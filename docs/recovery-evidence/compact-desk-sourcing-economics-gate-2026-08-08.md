# Compact desk and small-space organization sourcing economics gate

**Evidence date:** 2026-08-08

**Scope:** passive desk organizers, cable management, monitor risers/shelves, bundles, and the conditional rechargeable lamp

**Primary launch model:** Canada, CAD; US prices are competitive context and provisional USD ceilings

**Action status:** evidence only; no catalog, price, shipping, discount, supplier or publication changes were made.

## Executive rule

The cluster should not be built around isolated CAD 8–15 commodity products. Cable clips and ties are useful **add-ons**, but their mass-retail price leaves too little landed-cost headroom for paid acquisition or separate dropship shipping.

The commercially coherent offer is a **CAD 60–75 desk-reset bundle** assembled from passive, visually demonstrable products:

- cable tray or cable box;
- compact organizer/caddy;
- cable kit/clips;
- optionally, only a verified low-cost lamp route.

At a 15% promotion, CAD 60 becomes CAD 51.00 and still clears a CAD 50 threshold if the threshold uses the discounted subtotal. A list subtotal below **CAD 58.83** becomes less than CAD 50 after 15% off. Therefore CAD 60 is the minimum safe advertised bundle level under that stress case; CAD 70–75 provides healthier room.

## Formula and required cost definition

This uses the established conservative model:

- payment fee: 3.5% of merchandise revenue + 0.30 in the sale currency;
- refund/defect reserve: 5% of merchandise revenue;
- required contribution: 30% of merchandise revenue;
- 15% promotion stress case;
- customer-paid shipping/duties contribute zero until checkout evidence proves retained revenue and responsibility.

```text
discounted revenue = advertised retail × 85%
maximum all-in landed cost = discounted revenue × 61.5% - 0.30
```

For a bundle, the maximum applies to the **sum** of:

- every item cost;
- every supplier's tracked shipping charge;
- order/automation charges;
- packaging/handling;
- any Puchica-paid duty, brokerage or delivery charge.

If three items ship independently, all three shipping charges count. The apparent AOV benefit can disappear quickly in a multi-supplier bundle.

### Lamp-bundle warning

At a CAD 75 list bundle, 15% off creates a CAD 38.91 total landed-cost pool. With a CAD 12 shipping reserve and a CAD 18.32 lamp item cost, only **CAD 8.59** remains for the tray, clips, automation/handling and any other charges. A lamp bundle is therefore not automatically profitable merely because AOV is higher.

## Free-shipping threshold implications

The recovery records repeatedly communicate “free Canadian shipping on orders over CAD 50,” but the exact live rate/discount configuration still requires checkout verification.

[Shopify documents price-based free-shipping rates](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/setup/shipping-rates/setting-up-shipping-rates) and warns that multiple shipping profiles can combine rates. [Shopify also documents that product discounts apply before order discounts and shipping discounts apply last](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/discounts/discount-combinations). The exact Puchica behavior depends on whether free shipping is a price-based shipping rate, an automatic discount, its combination settings, market currency conversion and which shipping profiles the products occupy.

Required tests before advertising the threshold:

1. Canada cart at CAD 49.99, 50.00 and 50.01 without a discount.
2. CAD 60 cart with the 15% promotion applied, producing CAD 51.00 merchandise revenue.
3. CAD 55 cart with the promotion, producing CAD 46.75.
4. One-product and multi-product carts across every relevant shipping profile.
5. A US-market cart to confirm converted threshold and combined-rate behavior.

Until those pass, do not assume a list-price subtotal qualifies after discount and do not credit customer shipping revenue in the economics model.

## Sourcing scorecard

A candidate advances only if every required field is present:

| Gate | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Exact identity | Supplier URL/item ID, exact option name/image, dimensions, materials, pack count |
| Ordinary cost | Repeatable price excluding welcome, coin, coupon and flash offers |
| Canada route | Tracked shipping cost, ETA, carrier/method, source warehouse, timestamp |
| US route | Same route evidence separately; no inference from Canada |
| Inventory | At least 25 units for the exact option, preferably materially more for an ad candidate |
| Economics | All-in landed cost at or below the high-band 15%-discount ceiling |
| Pricing resilience | Preferably at or below the low/mid-band ceiling, not barely under the maximum |
| Bundle fit | Compatible style/use case; same supplier/warehouse preferred; no hidden multiplied shipping |
| Claims | Every size, capacity, mounting, material and performance claim evidenced |
| Safety | Passive products preferred; lamp needs electrical/battery/charger documentation |
| Returns | Return path, defect handling and customer-facing policy aligned |

## Automatic reject rules

Reject without further optimization when any of these is true:

1. **Economics fail:** item cost alone exceeds the high-band 15%-discount landed ceiling.
2. **Shipping trap:** item cost fits, but tracked shipping/fees push total above the ceiling.
3. **Welcome-price dependence:** economics require a conditional/new-customer/coupon price.
4. **Commodity mismatch:** proposed retail materially exceeds IKEA/Target/Staples/Walmart without visible, evidenced differentiation.
5. **Bundle multiplication:** the bundle needs two or more independent shipping charges and no consolidated route can keep the combined cost inside the bundle pool.
6. **Threshold fragility:** the promoted cart drops below the free-shipping threshold or only works if free shipping is configured in an unverified way.
7. **Bulky low-value product:** dimensional shipping consumes the margin on a caddy, drawer or monitor stand.
8. **Unverified mounting/load claims:** clamp thickness, tray capacity, adhesive type, monitor load or dimensions are missing.
9. **Electrical evidence gap:** a lamp lacks exact battery, charging input, included cable/adapter, certification/safety and transport evidence.
10. **Unsupported creative:** only supplier infographics exist, or images/copy imply features not proven by the exact option.
11. **Inventory weakness:** exact option stock is below 25 or unstable.
12. **Margin rescued by inflated pricing:** the route works only at a price outside the defensible competitive band.

## Recommended sourcing order

1. **Compact cable tray/box** with low dimensional shipping and clear installation evidence.
2. **Compact desktop organizer** that visibly solves a small-space problem and ships efficiently.
3. **Cable-management kit** designed as an add-on or bundle component.
4. **Monitor stand/shelf** only if load, dimensions and shipping remain favorable.
5. **Cable clips/ties** only as add-ons, multipacks or bundle value—not as ad-led standalone products.
6. **Lamp** only after an exact low-cost mapped option passes route and safety evidence.
