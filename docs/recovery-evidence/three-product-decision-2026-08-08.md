## Desk-lamp gate

The lamp is not a launch product yet. It may advance only when all of the
following are captured for `Big Recharge 3color`:

1. ordinary repeatable item cost for that exact option;
2. exact colour, dimensions, charging connector, cable/adapter contents,
   battery chemistry/capacity, charge time, runtime and three-light behaviour;
3. product/model label and applicable Canadian electrical/battery safety
   evidence;
4. exact-option media and usage rights;
5. all-in landed cost no higher than CA$20.61 under the high-band 15%-discount
   stress case, with a lower target preferred;
6. a rebuilt Shopify variant rather than `Default Title`; and
7. a defensible retail price within the competitive CA$22–40 range.

If the exact option cannot clear those gates, reject it without further design
or copy work.
