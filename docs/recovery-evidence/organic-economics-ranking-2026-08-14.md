## Repeatable check

```powershell
npm run organic-economics
```

The command reads live localized Storefront prices, verifies every exact SKU
against the approved CA/U.S. catalog, and combines them with the dated exact
DSers cost and tracked-route baseline. It fails closed when the supplier
evidence is older than seven days or no longer covers the complete approved
market/SKU set.

## Assumptions and limits

- Single-item checkout shipping: CA$5 in Canada and US$8 in the United States.
- Payment reserve: 3.5% of collected total plus 0.30 in the order currency.
- Refund/exception reserve: 5% of collected total.
- Canadian supplier conversion: CA$1.40 per US$1.
- Supplier item cost and tracked route: exact DSers evidence dated 2026-08-14.
- The cable and toiletry checkout totals were freshly verified in both markets.
  Supporting-offer rows use the configured storewide single-item shipping rate
  and are planning estimates, not a complete address-matrix result.
- Contribution is before advertising, income tax, unexpected duty/brokerage,
  customer-service labor, and any unreserved exception.
- Every supplier cost, stock signal, and destination route must be rechecked
  before a genuine supplier payment.

## Paid-ad boundary

Every offer remains `HOLD` for paid advertising until the normal-browser
analytics receipt/deduplication trace, one genuine delivered order, Purchase
event verification, physical-product outcome, and actual delivered
contribution all pass. The positive planned margins authorize controlled
organic selling, not spend.
