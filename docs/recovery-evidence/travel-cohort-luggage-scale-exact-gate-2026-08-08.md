# Travel cohort exact gate — LCD luggage scale — 2026-08-08

## Hard decision

**REJECT for the launch cohort and paid acquisition.**

Option B has usable stock and inexpensive tracked shipping, but this is a highly commoditized scale whose defensible retail price is too close to landed cost. More importantly, the two customer options are only named `A` and `B`, the supplier description does not document what physically differs, and the claimed 10 g resolution/accuracy has no manual, calibration tolerance, or test basis in DSers. No sample purchase or paid validation is required; the rejection is based on existing documentary, inventory, route, and market evidence.

## Battery and safety basis

The CR2032 is not included, so the supplier route does not ship a battery. A future listing would still need to tell customers which battery to install and warn that button batteries must be kept away from children. Health Canada says swallowed button batteries can cause serious injury or death and recommends secure battery compartments: https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/household-products/battery-safety/button.html

DSers exposes no evidence that this scale's battery compartment is screw-secured. Do not market it for children or show loose CR2032 cells in creative.

## Final rationale

Reject because all four gates do not align:

1. **Identity:** A/B are opaque and cannot be safely merchandised.
2. **Truth:** 10 g resolution and measurement accuracy are not adequately documented.
3. **Economics:** landed CA$13.40–13.80 is too high for the commodity retail band.
4. **Differentiation:** there is no feature, brand, bundle, or content advantage capable of supporting paid acquisition.

Do not spend route-audit, copywriting, or storefront time on this SKU unless the supplier later supplies a clear A/B comparison, a manual with accuracy/tolerance, and materially better landed economics. No purchase is needed to reach this decision.
