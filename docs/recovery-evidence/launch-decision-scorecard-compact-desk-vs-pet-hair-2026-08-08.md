# Launch decision scorecard — compact desk vs pet-hair cleanup

**Decision date:** 2026-08-08
**Scope:** Canada + United States; evidence only; no catalog or advertising changes

## Non-negotiable GO gate

A niche is **GO for storefront rebuild and advertising preparation only when every threshold below passes**. A weighted score cannot compensate for a failed route, margin, fidelity, or safety gate.

| Gate | Required GO threshold |
|---|---|
| Supplier availability | **3 exact products** from the same niche, each with exact-option mapping, ordinary repeatable price, stock, recent order/review evidence, and tracked **Canada and US** route cost/ETA. No visual substitutes, Welcome Deals, or coupon economics. |
| Landed economics | Each exact SKU passes its product-specific all-in landed ceiling after a **15% customer discount**, using the established payment-fee/reserve model and preserving **30% contribution**. The three-product bundle must also pass after multiplied shipping is applied. |
| Three-product coherence | One customer, one problem sequence, and one landing-page story; each product has a distinct job. No unrelated “trending” filler. |
| AOV | A credible acquisition bundle of at least **CA$59 / US$45**, priced within ordinary market bands, with no invented compare-at price. |
| Ad demonstration | At least **2 reproducible exact-product demonstrations** with a visible problem/result in 15–30 seconds; claims, fit and included parts can be shown honestly with the customer SKU. |
| Return / safety | Passive, unbranded products; exact dimensions/materials/limitations documented; no unsupported universal-fit, damage-free, fabric-safe, load or performance claims. Modeled fit/performance failure does not erase contribution margin. |
| Time to launch | The three-SKU evidence packet, pages, CA/US checkout QA and creative can reasonably reach release review in **14 calendar days or less** without lowering any prior gate. |

## Applied scorecard

`PASS` means current evidence meets the threshold; `CONDITIONAL` means the concept works but exact products do not yet prove it; `FAIL` is a launch blocker.

| Gate | Compact desk / small-space organization | Pet-hair / upholstery cleanup |
|---|---|---|
| Supplier availability | **FAIL — 0/3 approved.** The 18-clip exact ID is blocked because DSers exposed no exact CA/US route. Three public cable-tray IDs fail the CA$18 tray ceiling on item cost alone. No vertical-stand or drawer-set route has passed. | **FAIL — 0/3 approved.** Current evidence contains a concept and retail comparators, but no exact supplier IDs, options, ordinary prices, reviews, or CA/US route captures for a roller, crevice tool and travel brush. |
| CA/US landed economics | **FAIL today, but governed.** Exact ceilings and bundle math already exist: routing kit CA$12.77; tray CA$18.00; the proposed Cable Reset is CA$59–69. Current tray leads fail and the clip route is unknown, but the team knows exactly what a replacement must beat. | **FAIL and unmodeled.** Generic competition is roughly CA$5–19, with known/branded rollers around CA$26–41. No exact landed-cost model proves that three generic tools can support competitive retail plus paid acquisition in both markets. |
| Three-product coherence | **PASS at architecture level.** Cable containment → device/document storage → drawer organization is one small-desk reset, and every proposed item has a separate job. | **CONDITIONAL.** Full-size roller → crevice detailer → travel brush is coherent, but performance overlap is high and no exact cohort proves differentiated jobs. |
| AOV | **PASS conditionally.** Existing offer architecture supports CA$59–69 for Cable Reset and CA$84–99 with a vertical stand, subject to exact landed economics. | **FAIL on evidence.** A three-tool bundle might raise AOV, but current low commodity retail bands and absent costs do not prove CA$59 / US$45 without an unsupported premium. |
| Ad-demonstration strength | **PASS conceptually.** Cords off the floor, reclaimed desk depth and a measured fit/install sequence support strong before/after creative. Exact-SKU media is still required before ads. | **PASS conceptually.** Fur removal is instantly legible and demonstrable, but results vary by fur and fabric; exact-product testing is mandatory. |
| Return / safety risk | **CONDITIONAL / lower risk.** Passive products avoid batteries, apps and electrical rules. Main risks—clamp fit, adhesive residue, load and dimensions—are measurable and can be screened before sale. | **CONDITIONAL / higher claim risk.** Passive tools are physically simple, but fur type, weave and fabric condition create variable performance and “fabric safe”/universal claims invite dissatisfaction. |
| Time to launch | **CONDITIONAL: 10–14 days after three routes pass.** Niche, offer, economics formula, page architecture and creative plan already exist. Sourcing is the critical path. | **FAIL: likely more than 14 days.** It would restart exact sourcing, economics, product testing, copy, merchandising and creative from zero while discarding completed desk strategy. |

## Result

Neither niche is **GO for advertising today** because neither has three exact, route-verified, economically approved products. The comparison is nevertheless decisive:

- Compact desk has a proven strategic fit, a coherent three-product story, explicit Canada/US economics gates, a plausible CA$59+ acquisition bundle, and completed merchandising/creative architecture. Its blocker is narrow: **replace failed suppliers and prove three exact routes**.
- Pet-hair cleanup has strong demonstration potential but no supplier cohort, no landed model, no proven AOV, higher performance-claim risk, and would restart the launch clock.

## One recommended action

**Do not pivot and do not unpause ads. Commit exclusively to compact-desk organization, freeze all pet-hair work, and run one bounded replacement-sourcing sprint until three passive exact SKUs pass every GO threshold above.** Replace the uneconomic cable tray rather than relaxing the CA$18 ceiling; retain the routing-kit role only if an exact DSers route appears; source the vertical stand and drawer organizer in parallel. If three products do not pass within the 12-candidate-per-slot stop rule, keep the store contained and change the product role—not the niche or evidence standard.
