## Cohort implication

The route-capable products do not automatically form one coherent store:

- There is no verified grooming pair. The mini hair tool is blocked on voltage,
  while the straightener brush remains conditional on opaque SKU `03`.
- The milk frother belongs to home/kitchen and should be judged separately. Its
  economics are too narrow to justify using it merely to increase product count.
- BoyKeep, Essager and the drill must not be included in a Canada/US launch in
  their current mapped configurations.

Do not force all three route-capable products into one launch cohort. Move to a
coherent alternative cluster from the next-existing-product tranche while the
straightener brush and milk frother are evaluated independently.

## Immediate actions

1. Keep `Pink-US` blocked unless the exact current option provides an input
   label supporting 120V/60Hz or 100–240V/50–60Hz. Plug shape is insufficient.
2. Resolve supplier SKU `03` to its exact image, colour, battery, temperature
   settings, runtime, charger and included parts.
3. Calculate exact landed contribution for the straightener brush at defensible
   CA$40–50 retail scenarios. Keep duties/brokerage conservative until checkout
   evidence proves otherwise.
4. Inspect the next coherent existing-product tranche rather than approving a
   product merely to reach three.
5. Evaluate the milk frother independently at a competitive price. Reject or
   re-source it if full landed cost exceeds CA$15.38 or leaves no operational
   buffer.
6. Make no Shopify copy, image, option, price or publication change until an
   exact variant has a complete evidence record and passes preview review.
