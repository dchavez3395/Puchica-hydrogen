## Cohort implication

The route-capable products do not automatically form one coherent store:

- The mini hair tool and straightener brush could become a two-product grooming
  pair if their exact options, safety evidence, copy and margin pass.
- The milk frother belongs to home/kitchen and should be judged separately. Its
  economics are too narrow to justify using it merely to increase product count.
- BoyKeep, Essager and the drill must not be included in a Canada/US launch in
  their current mapped configurations.

Do not force all three route-capable products into one launch cohort. A third
grooming product should be inspected only if the first two survive their deeper
variant and compliance review. If they do not, move to a coherent alternative
cluster from the next-existing-product tranche.
