## 1. 500 mL temperature-display thermos

### Current Shopify evidence

- Product ID: `9351895548154`; SKU: `PUCHICA-1005007433246294`.
- One `Default Title` variant; CA$127.33 price; CA$191.00 compare-at price;
  CA$80.16 Shopify unit cost; placeholder inventory `50`.
- DSers product-card evidence: CA$37.32 item cost and 32,460 stock, but exact
  option and Canada route were not inspected.
- One 1200×896 lifestyle image. It shows a plain silver cylindrical bottle and
  a digital lid reading `-47°C`. It does not show the interior, a divider,
  packaging, dimensions, food-contact markings, warnings, colour option, or
  supplier identity.
- Shipping weight is zero; origin and HS code are missing.

The filename and composition are storefront-specific lifestyle media, not
captured supplier-option evidence. The image cannot be used to prove exact SKU
fidelity or product/image rights.

### Public verification and competitive position

- An exact supplier-ID mirror for `1005007433246294` exposes 500 mL as the
  capacity and multiple colour options (black, blue, bright red, green, pink,
  purple, red, and white), with a public price range of AU$6.69–17.72:
  [Kambo mirror](https://kambo.com.au/product/500ml-temperature-display-thermos-cup-stainless-steel-divided-thermos-bottle-outdoor-vacuum-insulated-cup-fathers-day-gift/).
- A Canadian marketplace listing with the same exact long title showed
  CA$26.99 with free seller delivery:
  [Walmart Canada](https://www.walmart.ca/en/ip/500ML-Temperature-Display-Thermos-Cup-Stainless-Steel-Divided-Thermos-Bottle-Outdoor-Vacuum-Insulated-Cup-Father-s-Day-Gift/2I2IJEWNO9HL).
- Other exact-title public offers were about US$13.99–40.99. These are useful
  market observations, not evidence that their quality or route equals the
  mapped DSers option.

Puchica’s CA$127.33 is about 4.7× the exact-title Canadian marketplace price.
Even the lower CA$37.32 DSers card cost is above that comparator before
shipping, fees, returns, and advertising. The CA$80.16 Shopify unit cost makes
the conflict worse. This is not a copy-only problem.

### Claims to remove now

- “divided two-section design”;
- “keep hot and cold drinks separate” and “stack a snack in the dry
  compartment”;
- “morning coffee still hot at lunch,” “cold water all afternoon,” and
  “boiling-hot tea on a cold summit”;
- “fits car cup holders and most backpack side pockets”;
- “33% saving while stock lasts” and the CA$191 compare-at claim;
- “sold consistently on AliExpress”;
- “verified supplier” as a quality/fidelity assurance.

The current image does not substantiate any of these. “Divided” in a supplier
title is not proof of an internal two-beverage compartment.

### Claims/evidence required for any replacement offer

- Exact DSers colour/option and one-to-one Shopify mapping.
- 500 mL usable capacity, dimensions, empty weight, lid construction, display
  power source, temperature range/accuracy, and display operating life.
- Exact food-contact materials for body, liner, lid, seals, coatings, and any
  plastics; supplier test documents tied to the model/lot.
- Vacuum-insulation performance under stated conditions; leak resistance;
  care, maximum fill temperature, hot-liquid warnings, and whether the product
  is dishwasher safe.
- Exact product/packaging photos, colour-specific media, rights to use them,
  origin, weight, HS code, and Canada landed cost.

Health Canada advises using food and drink products according to their intended
use and notes the material-dependent nature of transfer into food; stainless
steel composition alone is not evidence for this exact item:
[Health Canada cookware/material guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/household-products/safe-use-cookware.html).

### Final recommendation

**Reject the current offer and mapped economics.** Do not spend more time
rewriting it unless a different exact supplier can provide a landed cost and
evidence packet that supports a competitive selling price. The present hero
image and Default Title variant should not be reused as exact-product evidence.

## 2. USB desk lamp

### Current Shopify evidence

- Product ID: `9351895810298`; SKU: `PUCHICA-1005007009899251`.
- One `Default Title` variant; CA$138.22 price; CA$207.33 compare-at price;
  CA$86.99 Shopify unit cost; placeholder inventory `50`.
- DSers product-card evidence: CA$18.32–31.63 item-cost range and 24,973 stock,
  but exact option and Canada route were not inspected.
- One 1200×896 lifestyle image shows a white articulated LED lamp with a round
  base and touch icon. It does not show a charging port/cable, battery label,
  packaging, remote, certification mark, model number, or brightness controls
  in operation.
- Shipping weight is zero; origin and HS code are missing.

The image is polished lifestyle media, but it is not sufficient evidence that
the mapped DSers SKU has this form, colour, battery, interface, or included
parts.

### Public verification and competitive position

- An exact-title family mirror lists “Table Lamp” and “Table Lamp Remote” as
  separate options at US$39.40 and describes ABS/PVC construction,
  rechargeable battery, touch control, and three levels:
  [Afralia exact-title mirror](https://afralia.com/products/afralia%E2%84%A2-led-desk-lamp-3-levels-dimmable-touch-night-light-usb-rechargeable-foldable-table-lamp).
  It does **not** prove Puchica’s mapped option or that a remote is included.
- A close Canadian three-level rechargeable touch lamp was CA$21.87 with free
  seller delivery:
  [Walmart Canada comparator](https://www.walmart.ca/en/ip/USB-Rechargeable-LED-Desk-Lamp-3-Level-Dimmable-Touch-Table-Light-for-Bedroom-Bedside/6RIA67SQ0RXD).
- Other current Canadian marketplace comparables observed during this audit
  ranged roughly from CA$17 to CA$45 depending on form, battery, and delivery.

Puchica’s CA$138.22 has no established branded, certified, warranty, or design
advantage that could support a three-to-six-times generic-market premium. The
CA$86.99 Shopify cost and CA$18.32–31.63 DSers card range must be reconciled
before any margin calculation.

### Claims to remove now

- “low for bedside reading, medium for focused desk work, and high for video
  calls or craft projects”;
- “built-in rechargeable cell” and cordless use;
- “plug into any USB port, dock, or power bank to run it indefinitely”;
- “no outlet required”;
- “5,000+ units sold” and “strong seller”;
- “verified suppliers” as a product assurance;
- “lightweight,” “single-piece cable,” plane/train use, and every implied
  included-accessory claim;
- “33% saving while stock lasts” and the CA$207.33 compare-at claim.

Three brightness levels and touch control are plausible at the family level,
but still require exact-option proof before publication.

### Claims/evidence required for any replacement offer

- Exact DSers option, including lamp versus remote version, colour, connector,
  cable, adapter, and all included parts.
- Model/label photos; dimensions/weight/materials; LED power, colour
  temperature, brightness levels/lumens, control behaviour, and adjustability.
- Battery chemistry/capacity, charging input voltage/current, charge time,
  runtime by brightness, protection circuitry, transport documentation, and
  English/French warnings/manual.
- Applicable Canadian electrical safety/certification evidence tied to the
  exact model, plus a photo showing the mark on the product where required.
- Exact-product gallery and media rights; origin, HS code, shipping weight,
  route, landed cost, defect/warranty reserve, and competitive target price.

Health Canada warns that online electrical products may pose shock, burn, fire,
or explosion hazards and advises checking Canadian certification marks and
charger compatibility. Rechargeable lithium-ion products also bring
overheating/fire risk:
[online electrical product safety](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/topics/consumer-product-safety-education/online-shopping/electrical-products.html),
[lithium-ion battery safety](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/household-products/battery-safety/lithium-ion.html).

### Final recommendation

**Hold only as a product concept. Reject the current mapped SKU/price until
re-proved.** It may be repairable if DSers shows the exact option, Canada route,
certification/safety packet, and a cost that supports a realistic CA$30–50
position. Otherwise reject it without further creative work.

## 3. Naturehike toiletry bag

### Current Shopify evidence

- Product ID: `9341750968570` with six coded variants: `BK-L`, `Grey-L`,
  `BK-S`, `BN-S`, `BN-L`, and `Grey-S`.
- The option is incorrectly named `Color` even though each code combines colour
  and size. Brown variants exist, while all alt text says only “Black or Grey.”
- Prices span CA$40.23–73.45; Shopify unit cost is CA$23.32 for every variant.
  Per-variant inventory is only 4–12.
- Twelve images show a zippered rectangular hanging toiletry case in grey,
  black, and brown. The hero visibly carries Naturehike branding.
- The description instead sells an 8/15 L roll-top dry bag with shoulder strap,
  MOLLE attachment, 500D PVC tarpaulin/TPU, IPX6 protection, YKK zippers, and
  dimensions of 28/35 cm. That is a different product architecture.
- Origin and HS code are missing. All 12 alt texts are duplicated, do not
  distinguish the image purpose or variant, and omit Brown.

### Public verification and competitive position

Naturehike Canada’s public **Shower toiletry bag** closely matches the Shopify
media: a zip case in 22×14×8 cm and 24×16×9.5 cm sizes. Naturehike describes it
as high-density nylon with a waterproof coating, dry/wet separation, 176 g,
and model `NH18X030-B`. It does not describe a roll top, 8/15 L capacity,
shoulder strap, MOLLE, IPX6/submersion, 500D PVC/TPU, or the Shopify dimensions.
The page lists CA$10.19 against CA$16.99:
[Naturehike Canada shower toiletry bag](https://naturehike.ca/en/products/toilet-shower-kit).

Naturehike Canada’s separate TPU mesh bag is only 1.2/2.6 L, further showing
that “TPU” or a Naturehike name does not validate Puchica’s 8/15 L claim:
[Naturehike Canada TPU mesh bag](https://naturehike.ca/en/collections/sale/products/toiletry-bag-net-tpu).

Puchica is asking roughly four to seven times Naturehike Canada’s current price
for the visually matching product family, before establishing authorized
resale, warranty support, or faster local fulfilment. The CA$23.32 Shopify unit
cost itself exceeds the official Canadian retail price observed.

### Claims to remove now

Remove the entire existing feature/specification story, specifically:

- “100% waterproof,” IPX6, and accidental submersion protection;
- 8 L/15 L capacity and 28×16×20 / 35×20×25 cm dimensions;
- roll-top/watertight closure and compression;
- adjustable shoulder strap and MOLLE attachment points;
- 500D PVC tarpaulin, TPU coating, reinforced seams, YKK zippers, and
  abrasion-resistance claims;
- 260/380 g weights;
- protecting phones/electronics “on the water,” saltwater protection, and
  exterior-backpack attachment;
- “TSA-friendly,” “globally trusted,” “full Canadian warranty support,” and
  “no questions asked” claims unless separately and operationally proven.

IPX6 concerns powerful water jets; it is not a submersion rating. The copy’s
own combination of IPX6 and “accidental submersion” is unsafe and misleading
even before exact-SKU verification.

### Variant, brand, and media gaps

- Capture the exact mapped supplier product and confirm whether it is genuine
  model `NH18X030-B`, another authentic Naturehike model, or an unauthorized
  logo-bearing copy.
- Obtain authorization/invoice chain for branded resale and image use. A logo
  in supplier media is not authorization.
- Map `BK`, `Grey`, and `BN` to customer-facing Black, Grey, and Brown, and split
  colour and size into separate options. Confirm every one-to-one DSers mapping.
- Resolve why Brown exists in Shopify/supplier media but does not appear in the
  matching Naturehike Canada model’s documented colours.
- Replace duplicated alt text with image-specific, non-promotional descriptions
  and attach variant media to the correct colour/size.
- Prove dimensions, material, weight, coating/water-resistance level,
  compartments, hanging hook, included items, care, and warranty for the exact
  model.

### Final recommendation

**Reject the current branded listing.** It is a confirmed different-product
copy mismatch and is commercially undercut by the brand’s Canadian storefront.
Do not salvage it by merely replacing the text. Only reconsider a newly built
listing after exact-model mapping, authorization, route/economics, and a
competitive price are established. A generic unbranded replacement would need
new logo-free media and should be treated as a separate product.

## Cross-product release gate

None of the three should enter the launch cohort until all of the following are
true:

1. exact DSers option and quantity are captured and one-to-one mapped;
2. Shopify cost and DSers cost discrepancy is resolved;
3. Canada shipping, delivery, tracking, duties, and returns are captured for
   the exact SKU;
4. landed contribution margin works at a competitive—not invented—price;
5. every title/body/spec/media claim has exact-model evidence;
6. safety/certification, brand authorization, origin, HS code, weight, and
   media rights are documented; and
7. product-specific and variant-specific media replace unsupported lifestyle or
   duplicate supplier imagery.
