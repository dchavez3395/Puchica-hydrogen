# Next existing-product tranche — 2026-08-08

## Decision

This is a **conditional inspection queue**, not a product approval list. If the
current five-product recovery cohort fails, inspect the six products below in
this order. None may be published, advertised, or described as launch-ready
until its exact DSers SKU, Canada route, delivered cost, product/media fidelity,
claims, and selling price pass the launch gates.

The queue was selected from the 23 mapped products that remained after removing
the six products already covered by the first content-fidelity audit. “Mapped”
means only that DSers shows a product-level supplier relationship; it does not
prove that the correct variant is mapped or that it can be fulfilled profitably.

## Selection principles

Priority was given to products that have:

1. a plausible place in a small number of understandable merchandising clusters;
2. a storefront/supplier mismatch that appears repairable by honest copy, media,
   option, or naming work rather than by inventing a different product;
3. less regulatory, health-claim, app, firmware, or technical-support exposure
   than the rest of this catalog;
4. enough item-cost-only headroom to justify a route/economics inspection; and
5. non-trivial supplier stock in the currently captured DSers evidence.

Item-cost-only headroom is **not margin**. It excludes shipping, duties,
brokerage, tax treatment, payment fees, refunds, defects, discounts, and ads.

## Cluster recommendation

Do not put all six on one storefront merely because they survive inspection.
Use the evidence to form one coherent launch story:

- **Primary cluster: compact routines for home and travel** — thermos, desk lamp,
  toiletry bag. This is the cleanest low-support grouping, subject to food-contact,
  battery, and mapping evidence.
- **Secondary test cluster: purpose-built utility** — phone holder, knives,
  hammock. These are backups only; each brings a distinct fit, trademark, sharp-
  object, or load-bearing risk.

If fewer than three products pass within one cluster, do not force a broad
catalog launch. Keep the store contained and source a more coherent replacement
cohort.

## Required inspection packet for each queued candidate

Before any candidate can be promoted, record all of the following in the
recovery evidence folder:

1. Shopify product ID, variant ID, option values, and current publication state.
2. DSers product ID, supplier URL, exact mapped SKU/quantity/colour/size, and a
   screenshot showing the mapping.
3. Canada shipping method, origin, cost, ETA, tracking, delivery guarantee, and
   return/refund terms for the exact SKU.
4. Exact item cost plus shipping, expected duties/brokerage/tax treatment,
   payment fees, defect/refund reserve, discount impact, and target contribution
   margin at a competitive selling price.
5. A claim-to-evidence table covering title, description, specifications,
   included items, dimensions, materials, compatibility, images, and options.
6. Supplier stock freshness, brand/image authorization, product-safety evidence,
   sample requirement, and a go/hold/reject decision with owner and date.
