supplier, changing Shopify Admin, or spending on advertising.

## Why this architecture wins

- All five products solve one recognizable travel-organization job.
- Soft goods are compact, light, non-electrical, and relatively low-breakage.
- The collection supports packing, bathroom, shoe/laundry, technology, and
  document-access demonstrations without changing niche.
- Complements create a credible cross-sell and bundle ladder.
- The current packing-cube record carries the strongest provisional US evidence
  in the existing catalog.
- A single campaign can promote the hero or starter set while the remaining
  products provide storefront depth and additional order value.

The principal risk is commodity pressure. Puchica must compete with proof,
curation, exact information, consistent imagery, and a coherent offer rather
than generic supplier claims or artificial discounts.

## Exact SKU gate

Every one of the five must independently prove:

- [ ] One Shopify product, one canonical sellable variant, and a clean
      customer-facing option name.
- [ ] One exact DSers supplier product, raw variant/SKU, and non-promotional
      repeat-order price.
- [ ] Supplier/product history, current stock at least 25 units, and no silent
      substitution or ambiguous warehouse/variant mapping.
- [ ] Current tracked route to ZIP 10001 and ZIP 90001, including item cost,
      shipping, service, ship-from, dispatch and delivery range.
- [ ] Country of origin, duty/tariff/brokerage treatment, importer responsibility
      and whether any charge can be demanded from the customer on delivery.
- [ ] Supplier return window and real return country/address process; damage,
      loss, wrong-item and change-of-mind responsibility.
- [ ] Exact contents, dimensions, weight, materials, care, limitations and what
      is not included.
- [ ] Neutral/approved packaging, no supplier advertising insert, and usable
      product-image rights.
- [ ] Current competitor benchmarks for a materially equivalent product.
- [ ] Worse-ZIP pre-ad contribution at least 30%; unknown cost is never zero.
- [ ] No unsupported performance, waterproof, RFID, safety, scarcity, review or
      compression-percentage claim.
- [ ] Final disposition `GO`, `HOLD`, or `REJECT` with dated evidence.

## Commercial controls

Use one currency and ordinary prices:

```text
net merchandise = retail x (1 - promotion)
payment cost = net merchandise x payment rate + fixed fee
landed supply = item + supplier shipping + duty/tariff/brokerage
                + automation + handling + packaging
problem reserve = net merchandise x reserve rate
pre-ad contribution = net merchandise - payment cost
                      - landed supply - problem reserve
break-even CAC = pre-ad contribution
initial target CAC = 70% x pre-ad contribution
```

Include the Canadian Shopify Payments foreign-currency cost in every USD row.
Do not count collected sales tax as revenue. Do not treat customer shipping as
margin unless its payout treatment and supplier cost are reconciled.

Planning bands, subject to exact evidence:

- hero: US$44-US$52;
- hero + toiletry complement: US$69-US$79;
- three-product travel reset: US$89-US$105;
