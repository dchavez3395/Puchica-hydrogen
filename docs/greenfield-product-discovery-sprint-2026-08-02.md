# Greenfield product discovery sprint — 2026-08-02

## Objective

Select a new Puchica hero and one backup without favoring the current catalog.
The niche remains practical small-space organization. No product is approved
until an exact supplier SKU passes both Canada and the United States.

## Non-negotiable product filter

- tracked normal shipping to Canada and the United States
- normal supplier pricing; no welcome, first-order, countdown, or coupon price
- enough margin for payment fees, FX, a 15% problem reserve and paid acquisition
- compact and light enough to avoid fragile/bulky fulfillment economics
- non-electrical, non-ingestible, non-medical and low safety/regulatory risk
- visually obvious before/after or demonstration creative
- limited size/fit ambiguity and manageable return-to-sender exposure
- no quality claim that requires unsupported testing

## Prioritized sourcing queue

   strong demand signal, clear problem and low breakage.
2. **Over-door fabric organizer** — best visual small-space transformation;
   advance only if packed shipping and door-fit evidence are clean.
3. **Magnetic spice-rack bundle** — strong kitchen reset story and bundle value;
   advance only with credible magnet and weight evidence.
4. **Under-sink pull-out organizer** — preserve as a high-visual backup, but
   reject quickly if dimensional or shipping economics are weak.

## Evidence required before storefront work

For the top three concepts, capture at least two established supplier candidates
and record exact option, supplier SKU, ordinary cost, stock, Canada route, U.S.
route, tracking, delivery, customer competitor band and provisional landed
margin. Reject any candidate with only country-level marketing copy, a blank
cost, promotional pricing, weak sales history, no route, or unverifiable fit.

After one exact SKU passes, create the product page and creative brief from that
actual item. Do not design another speculative landing page first.
