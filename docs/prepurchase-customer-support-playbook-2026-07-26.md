# Puchica Pre-Purchase Customer Support Playbook

Use this playbook for the first launch orders. Replace bracketed fields with verified order information. Never promise a delivery date, refund, or replacement before confirming the order record and applicable store policy.

## Response standards

- Acknowledge customer messages within one business day.
- Verify the order number, email, fulfillment status, tracking, and delivery address before answering.
- Keep all decisions and supplier evidence in the Shopify order timeline.
- Do not expose supplier names, internal costs, DSers screenshots, or internal margin notes.
- Escalate payment disputes, safety complaints, legal threats, and suspected fraud immediately.

## Escalation rules

- Payment captured but no Shopify order: stop and escalate to Shopify Payments support.
- Order not visible in DSers within 30 minutes: confirm product and variant mapping, then escalate to DSers support.
- Supplier requests a price adjustment or says the variant is unavailable: do not pay automatically; reassess margin and contact the customer if fulfillment cannot proceed.
- Tracking not created within the promised handling window: open a supplier inquiry and set a dated follow-up.
- Safety, injury, electrical, child-product, or allergic-reaction complaint: stop normal scripting, preserve evidence, unpublish the affected item if warranted, and escalate.
- Chargeback or fraud warning: preserve all order, tracking, customer, and address evidence; do not issue duplicate refunds.
