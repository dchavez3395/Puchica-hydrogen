## DSers research brief for new products

Look for products that are:

- Small/light, non-electrical, non-branded, non-medical and not child-safety
  equipment.
- Useful at a glance and demonstrable in a short product video.
- Under roughly CA$8 source cost before shipping, with the ability to sell
  around CA$24.99–39.99 without a fake compare-at price.
- Available for Canada first, with a tracked shipping option and stable stock.
- Naturally suited to Home & Kitchen, organization, pet enrichment, travel or
  everyday lifestyle—not broad apparel or novelty clutter.

Reject products with supplier warnings, zero stock, unclear variants, large or
fragile parcels, trademarked characters/brands, medical claims, electrical
compliance risk, or child safety claims.

## Approval checklist

1. Confirm exact DSers-to-Shopify variant mapping and add a Shopify SKU.
2. Record source price, Canada shipping, shipping method, delivery estimate,
   tracking availability and stock.
3. Price using landed cost plus payment fee, a discount allowance and an
   expected-return buffer. Paid candidates must clear 2.5× landed cost; 3× is
   preferred.
4. Create clean product copy, images, option names, tags, collection placement
   and SEO before publishing.
5. Run a no-payment checkout quote for Canada; only then consider a US quote
   and market launch.
