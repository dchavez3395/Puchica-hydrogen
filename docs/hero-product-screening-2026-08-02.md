## Provisional commercial threshold

The public reference band supports testing a normal retail hypothesis near
US$39.99–44.99 only if the exact four-piece product is comparable. At US$39.99,
using a 2.8% + $0.30 planning fee and 15% return/problem reserve:

- a US$15.32 landed supplier cost leaves about US$17.25 contribution, or 43.1%,
  before app allocation, FX, support, chargebacks, and other missing costs;
- a US$19.43 landed supplier cost leaves about US$13.14 contribution, or 32.9%,
  before those missing costs and is too tight for paid acquisition.

Therefore the exact chosen option should ideally land at or below **US$16** in
the worse market. A hard provisional ceiling of **US$18 landed** applies until
all missing costs are filled. These are screening controls, not a live price or
spend approval.

benchmark immediately before pricing approval.

## Exact-proof checklist

- [ ] Open the strongest established supplier listing without relying on a
      welcome, coupon, or first-order price.
- [ ] Prove that the selected option is a four-piece magnetic rack set and
      record color, dimensions, materials, load guidance, included hardware,
      stock, and supplier SKU.
- [ ] Confirm the product is genuinely magnetic rather than adhesive-only and
      capture any surface-compatibility limitation.
- [ ] Capture exact option cost and tracked route to approved non-personal
      destinations in Canada and the United States.
- [ ] Confirm normal retail comparables are materially equivalent four-piece
      products rather than sponsored or clearance anomalies.
- [ ] Complete the missing-cost worksheet and require at least 30% contribution
      in the worse market, with practical room above that floor for paid CAC.
- [ ] Review supplier images and claims for copying, translation, safety, and
      quality risks before creating a Puchica listing or creative.

Only after every box passes may the lead move to `PRODUCT_SELECTED`, at which
point page copy, images, creative concepts, and measurement QA can be built for
that exact SKU. Importing or paying still requires separate owner authority.
