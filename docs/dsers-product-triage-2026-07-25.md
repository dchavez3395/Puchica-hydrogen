# DSers mapped-catalog triage — 2026-07-25

## Decision

The 57 DSers mappings are a supplier library, not a 57-product storefront plan.
Puchica will launch as a focused useful-lifestyle shop with a small, credible assortment.

No product moves live until its exact variant has a Canadian supplier-shipping quote, a positive margin after the Canada price adjustment, FIRST15, payment fees, and checkout shipping, and complete storefront content.

The first cohort exists to prove the order-to-fulfilment path, not to limit the business to a tiny catalog. Build toward a 30+ product lifestyle assortment across coherent departments, releasing approved products in batches as their exact variants, supplier shipping, margin, content, tags, and collection placement are verified. Do not bulk-publish untouched imports; do not artificially stop after the first cohort either.

## Hold — do not publish without a specific exception

- Hand-Controlled Mini RC Drone, RC Monster Truck, and RC Construction Vehicle Set: safety, age guidance, variant complexity, and shipping risk.
- All heated vest/jacket products: electrical safety, USB/voltage, battery/transport, claims, and return exposure.
- Most apparel beyond the short seasonal edit: Puchica should not become a generic men’s-fashion catalogue.
- Sun visor organizer, carabiner clips, hair clips, sunglasses, and bottle brush: possible add-ons, but not category-defining products; validate delivery economics first.
- Baby bath-toy storage: only reconsider if its material, load limits, suction performance, and child-safety presentation are verifiable.

## Exclude from the storefront

- Any product with a supplier SKU marked out of stock.
- Faux piercings, rabbit-fur keychains, generic watch/jewellery sets, and products described as "luxury brand".
- Michael Jackson printed apparel, Elsa costumes, and other products with likely unlicensed intellectual-property use.
- Baby head/back protectors, anti-fall pillows, nasal irrigators, and similar products that make safety or health-adjacent claims.
- Cotton swabs and nail-treatment products whose wording creates health, treatment, or hygiene-risk positioning.
- Duck/goose shoes and other low-trust novelty products that do not fit the store promise.

## Operational sequence

1. Verify Canada landed cost and estimated delivery for the initial cohort, then repeat the same gate for every subsequent batch.
2. Remove duplicate/poor-fit items from consideration; do not delete DSers mappings until a replacement or historical reference is unnecessary.
3. Prepare approved candidates as Shopify drafts with clean titles, approved claims only, images, tags, collections, SEO, and raw DSers option mapping retained.
4. Run the existing paid test order through fulfillment and tracking before adding traffic or enabling automated marketing.
5. Add a product only after its first batch has the same margin and delivery evidence.
