## Commercial thresholds

Use the conservative screening model:

```text
contribution = (0.821 x retail price) - 0.30 - landed cost
initial target CAC = 0.70 x contribution
```

This includes a 2.8% + $0.30 payment planning fee and a 15% return/problem
reserve, but it still excludes some market-specific costs. Therefore:

- at US$49 retail, target landed cost is no more than US$18, preferably lower;
- at US$45 retail, target landed cost is no more than US$15.22;
- a candidate should support at least US$12-US$15 practical target CAC in the
  worse market after exact costs are filled;
- the worse-market pre-ad contribution must remain at least 30%;
- welcome, coupon, clearance, and first-order prices are inadmissible.

## Exact approval gate

A product may move to `PRODUCT_SELECTED` only after all boxes are proved for one
exact option:

- [ ] One supplier listing and one exact SKU/configuration, with ordinary price.
- [ ] Current stock and credible supplier history; target rating near 4.7 or better.
- [ ] Tracked Canada and U.S. routes for the same SKU, normally within 15 days.
- [ ] Exact item, shipping, duty/brokerage, FX, fee, reserve, and app allocation.
- [ ] At least 30% contribution in the worse market and practical US$12-US$15 CAC room.
- [ ] Comparable normal retail prices support Puchica's intended price.
- [ ] Clear material, dimensions, contents, limitations, and care instructions.
- [ ] No avoidable patent, trademark, trade-dress, safety, or installation risk.
- [ ] A credible before/after creative concept without unsupported claims.
- [ ] No mismatch between supplier option, storefront copy, image, and ad promise.
