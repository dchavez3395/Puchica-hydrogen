# Puchica creative production stack and UGC test plan — 2026-08-01

## Decision

Use the tools already available for still-image development. Treat CreateUGC
as a promising **video prototype tool**, not a required subscription. Defer a
Higgsfield subscription until the existing stack demonstrably cannot produce a
needed video format.

No trial, subscription, generation credits, ad upload, publishing, or spend is
authorized by this plan.

## Recommended stack

### Exact product reference

The supplier images for Shopify variant `49961853026554` / `5PCS Set Red` are
the visual source of truth. Every generated asset must be compared against them
for:

- exactly five pieces;
- red color and light trim;
- proportions and handles;
- mesh/fabric panels;
- zipper placement and count; and
- accessories or contents that are not included.

If the generated product differs materially, reject the asset. Do not repair a
commercially important mismatch only with copy or a disclaimer.

### Still images

Primary tools:

1. Codex image generation for controlled concepts and edits.
2. Antigravity `agy` with Nano Banana Pro for parallel lifestyle exploration.
3. Exact supplier media as the accuracy fallback.

Follow the supplied Product Optimization Workflow in **manual, one-product
test mode**. Generate concepts first; do not attach or reorder Shopify media
until a human approves the exact-product comparison. Generated imagery may add
context but may not invent dimensions, pieces, materials, performance, or
included items.

### UGC-style video

CreateUGC is the first tool to evaluate because it is purpose-built for
e-commerce UGC-style ads, accepts a product link or uploaded details, supports
9:16 output, avatars, scripts, and editing, and offers a short trial. Official
materials currently describe monthly plans at US$26.90, US$39.90, and US$66.90
and a three-day trial. Credits reset monthly rather than rolling over.

Official sources:

- https://help.createugc.ai/en/articles/15504680-getting-started-create-ai-videos-and-manage-your-credits
- https://www.createugc.ai/faq/

The official pages conflict on whether Product in Hand is available on every
plan or only higher tiers. The generation confirmation screen is the source of
truth before spending credits. The trial may place a card pre-authorization
hold, so enrollment requires explicit owner approval.

## One-product CreateUGC prototype

If the owner later authorizes the trial, use only the exact five-piece red set
and stop before any paid renewal.

Create three 9:16 concepts:

1. **Pack by category** — Hook: “One suitcase. Five places for the things you
   keep digging for.” Show the exact set immediately, then category separation.
2. **Find it without unpacking** — Hook: “Stop emptying your suitcase for one
   shirt.” Demonstrate retrieving one category without an unsupported speed or
   performance claim.
3. **What actually arrives** — Hook: “Here’s the complete five-piece red set.”
   Make product clarity, not a fake testimonial, the selling idea.

Recommended test order after Antigravity review:

1. **What actually arrives** — strongest trust and offer-clarity opener.
2. **Pack by category** — clearest functional problem/solution demonstration.
3. **Find it without unpacking** — situational secondary angle.

Creative rules:

- 8–20 seconds for the first test.
- Product appears in the first second.
- One promise per video.
- Burned-in captions with safe margins.
- No fake personal ownership, delivery, or results claim from an AI avatar.
- No review language such as “I bought this” or “I’ve used this for months.”
- No waterproof, airline-approved, TSA-approved, guaranteed-delivery, or
  compression-percentage claim.
- End with the exact campaign message and “Shop now” CTA.

The avatar may present the product and its observable features. It must not
pretend to be a genuine customer or conceal that a testimonial was generated.

## Prototype acceptance gate

Approve a CreateUGC clip only when all are true:

- exact variant remains visually faithful frame by frame;
- hands do not merge into the product and zippers do not move or multiply;
- voice and lip sync are usable at normal playback speed;
- hook is understandable without sound;
- captions are accurate and readable;
- product/campaign/price/currency match the destination market;
- no unsupported testimonial, review, urgency, scarcity, or delivery claim;
- 9:16 master crops safely to 4:5 and 1:1 where required; and
- landing-page message match is preserved.

## Tool-buying rule

Do not buy both CreateUGC and Higgsfield for the first test. Evaluate one
CreateUGC prototype against the existing still-image concepts. Buy a tool only
when its approved output solves a specific creative gap and the expected test
volume uses the credits economically.
