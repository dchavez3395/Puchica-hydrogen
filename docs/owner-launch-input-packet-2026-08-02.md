## Refused-delivery recommendation

Recommended owner decisions:

- treat refusal/nonpayment of disclosed destination charges as change-of-mind
  unless caused by Puchica error;
- wait for final carrier disposition and parcel recovery before approving a
  refund;
- deduct only actual documented nonrecoverable outbound, return-to-sender,
  customs, brokerage, and carrier fees where law permits;
- promise no automatic refund when a parcel is abandoned, destroyed, or not
  recovered;
- Puchica bears costs caused by a wrong, damaged, defective, or incorrectly
  disclosed order;
- no restocking fee unless separately approved and disclosed.

## Paste-ready policy wording

### Duties, brokerage, and refused delivery

The customer is responsible for duties, import charges, brokerage, or other
destination-specific charges that are not collected at checkout. If a carrier
or destination authority requests payment, the customer is responsible for
paying or arranging those charges.

If a customer refuses delivery or does not pay a disclosed destination charge,
the carrier may hold, return, abandon, or dispose of the parcel. Contact
hello@puchica.ca promptly so we can review the carrier information. Unless the
refusal resulted from a Puchica error or rights that cannot be limited by law,
it is handled as a change-of-mind request.

When the parcel is returned to the destination authorized for that case,
Puchica will review its condition and the carrier’s final charges. Any approved
refund is issued to the original payment method. Original shipping and actual
nonrecoverable return-to-sender, duty, brokerage, or carrier charges billed to
Puchica may be deducted from the refund where permitted by law. If the parcel is
not returned or recoverable, Puchica will wait for the carrier’s final
disposition and explain the available resolution; no automatic refund is
promised before that review.

Puchica does not deduct return costs caused by a confirmed damaged, defective,
or incorrect item or by Puchica’s own error. This section does not limit rights
that cannot be excluded under applicable law. Do not send any parcel to the
business address, the address printed on the parcel, or a supplier address
unless Puchica specifically authorizes that destination for the case.
