# Catalog Scale Plan — Launch Blocker

## Outcome

Build a credible Puchica lifestyle catalog with **30+ sellable products**, not a six-to-ten-product storefront. The current DSers pool is a source inventory to audit; it is neither an automatic publish list nor a reason to stop after a small validation batch.

## Publication gate — required for every SKU

1. DSers source and exact Shopify variant are linked and can be ordered.
2. Supplier shipping service, landed cost, destination coverage, and delivery estimate are recorded for Canada; add U.S. coverage before U.S. publication.
3. Selling price clears the agreed margin floor after supplier cost, supplier shipping, payment fees, discount exposure, and Shopify shipping contribution.
4. Product information is customer-ready: clean title, factual description, no unsupported claims, variants/options, image set, SEO, tags, and appropriate collection.
5. The product is manually reviewed for restricted, safety-sensitive, electrical, counterfeiting, or poor-fit risk.

## Batches

| Batch | Target | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Validation cohort | 8–12 products across 3–4 departments | Prove mapping, paid order hand-off, tracking, and the content/pricing workflow. |
| Catalog foundation | 20–30 approved products | Give each core lifestyle department enough choice to feel intentional. |
| Launch catalog | 30+ approved products | Provide a credible assortment while keeping unverified DSers imports in Draft. |

## Department mix

Prioritize practical, low-return-risk products in Home & Kitchen, Pet Supplies, Beauty & Grooming accessories, and selected Sports & Outdoors. Add apparel only where size, material, variant mapping, and return risk are clear. Keep medical-claim, heated/electrical, child-safety, and likely branded/counterfeit items out unless a specific compliance review clears them.

## Operating rule

No customer sees a product until it passes the gate. No qualified product waits merely because an arbitrary ten-product count has been reached.
