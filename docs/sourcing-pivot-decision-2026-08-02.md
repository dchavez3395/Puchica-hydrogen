# Sourcing pivot decision - 2026-08-02

## Executive decision

Puchica will not change to an unrelated niche. The next validation cycle will
narrow acquisition and merchandising to **carry-on and travel organization**
while preserving the broader Puchica brand shell so the decision is reversible.

Puchica will stop open-ended DSers/AliExpress hero-product searching. Repeated
candidate failures are attributable primarily to welcome-only supplier prices,
ambiguous variants, weak Canada routes, commodity price pressure, and inadequate
paid-acquisition contribution. Those failures do not establish that the customer
problem has no demand.

The sourcing route order is:

1. Shopify Collective Canada private Discovery audit.
2. DropCommerce no-cost catalog audit if Collective fails.
3. Syncee browse audit only if both earlier routes fail.
4. Do not add Spocket or another AliExpress wrapper during this cycle.

No app installation, supplier connection, product import, paid trial, order, or
advertising spend is authorized by this decision record.

## Why travel organization

The cross-functional score was 72.1/100 for carry-on/travel, versus 62.7 for
desk/cable reset and 53.7 for a broad small-space catalog. Travel is competitive,
but it offers the best combination of coherent creative, compact parcels, low
breakage, cross-border fulfillment, bundle potential, existing site fit, and
reversible implementation.

The acquisition story is:

> Pack clearly, find things faster, and keep a carry-on organized.

The intended hero remains one exact, genuinely compressible packing system.
The existing soft travel cable pouch may support it. Toiletry, shoe, or laundry
separators may be considered only after supplier and bundle economics pass. Do
not send paid traffic to the tube squeezer, under-sink bin, or fixed cable strip.

## Shopify Collective result

Read-only Shopify App Store inspection recognized Puchica as a Canadian store,
described the app as popular with similar Canadian luggage/bag stores, and showed
an enabled `Install` action rather than an incompatibility warning. This proves
listing-level compatibility, not full eligibility or product viability.

Collective is free and provides inventory/pricing synchronization, order routing,
tracking synchronization, supplier payment after fulfillment, and configurable
shipping/return policies. Its decisive constraints are:

- a retailer connects only with suppliers in the same country and store currency;
- Puchica therefore needs a Canadian/CAD supplier;
- the supplier must explicitly serve both Canadian and U.S. customers;
- exact product price lists, retailer margins, inventory, tracking badges,
  shipping zones, and return terms are private inside installed Discovery;
- public U.S. Collective examples are not evidence for Puchica;
- supplier dependency and stockouts remain operational risks.

Collective's typical 20-50% margin band is not automatically ad-viable. Under the
current 2.8% + C$0.30 payment planning fee and 15% reserve:

- 40% retailer margin supports only about US$6.78 target CAC at US$45 retail;
- 45% margin needs roughly US$64 retail to support US$12 target CAC;
- 50% margin needs roughly US$54 retail to support US$12 target CAC;
- every dollar of supplier shipping absorbed by Puchica removes about $0.70 from
  target CAC.

The practical Collective gate is therefore 50% margin for most US$55-70 heroes.
Forty percent is acceptable only near US$80 with proven shipping economics.

Status: `COLLECTIVE_CANADA_BLOCKED_PENDING_PRIVATE_DISCOVERY`.

## Alternative supplier routes

### DropCommerce - second

DropCommerce is the strongest geographic fallback because it focuses on Canadian
and U.S. suppliers and exposes supplier cost, minimum retail, country shipping,
processing/transit estimates, inventory, and fees. Publicly reported typical
delivery is two to seven days within North America, but every exact SKU still
requires warehouse, ship-to, duties, and return-address proof.

Free access is suitable for browsing/samples and up to 25 imports but not live
order processing. Basic is currently US$29/month. Do not start a trial or pay
until the free catalog exposes at least two qualifying exact products.

Status: `FALLBACK_BROWSE_ONLY`.

### Syncee - third

Syncee has a broader catalog and stronger price-rule tools, but live ordering is
less automatic: supplier orders require separate approval/payment and suppliers
control warehouse, shipping, returns, and responsiveness. Current entry pricing
is approximately US$39.99/month after limited free/browse access.

Status: `SECOND_FALLBACK_ONLY`.

### Spocket - reject for this cycle

Spocket starts near US$39.99/month, has the weakest current negative-review share
of the three compared routes, and does not eliminate supplier-specific shipping
or return risk.

Status: `DO_NOT_ADD`.

## Exact product gate

No product advances unless one exact SKU proves all of the following:

- [ ] Canadian/CAD or otherwise approved supplier relationship.
- [ ] Explicit tracked service to both Canada and the United States.
- [ ] No worse than 12 business days to the U.S. and 15 to Canada.
- [ ] Retail equivalent US$55-80 and honest everyday price support.
- [ ] Landed gross margin at least 55% for the travel validation offer.
- [ ] Pre-ad contribution at least US$25 / roughly C$34.
- [ ] Exact product, supplier shipping, fees, FX, duties, app allocation, and an
      8-15% defect/refund reserve recorded.
- [ ] Inventory at least 25 units; 100 preferred.
- [ ] Credible supplier/product reviews and carrier-verified tracking.
- [ ] Supplier-handled, documented return process and loss/damage remedy.
- [ ] Neutral or approved packaging with no supplier promotions.
- [ ] Exact piece count, dimensions, materials, packaging, image rights, and IP
      screen.
- [ ] For compression: a distinct second perimeter zipper that compresses a
      gusset, not merely a normal packing cube described as compressible.
- [ ] A strong three-second demonstration without invented performance claims.

## Fourteen-day stop rule

The sourcing pivot is time-boxed. Stop or change direction if, within 14 days of
authorized catalog access, Puchica cannot prove one exact hero and one supporting
product with both-market delivery, contribution, returns, inventory, image, and
IP evidence.

If Collective fails, run the DropCommerce free browse audit. If that also fails,
run one Syncee browse audit. If all three fail, stop travel hero sourcing and
reassess desk/cable reset without rebuilding the entire storefront first.

## Owner decision required

The next step requires authorization to install **Shopify Collective only** for
a private Discovery catalog audit. Installation grants the app access to store,
customer, order, product, analytics, settings, and payment-related data described
on its Shopify App Store listing. Approval to install is not approval to connect
a supplier, import a product, activate a trial, publish, order, or spend.

Before using any supplier, verify current Shopify Payments classification and
Collective retailer eligibility. Do not change the owner from `Individual` to a
registered business classification without matching registration documents.
