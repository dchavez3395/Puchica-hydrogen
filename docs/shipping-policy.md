# Shipping Policy — Puchica

> **Owner review required before publishing.** This draft contains no
> placeholders and reflects the exact catalog and route evidence reviewed on
> 2026-08-13. Checkout remains the source of truth for each order.

## Where we currently ship

Puchica currently serves Canada and the United States. Product availability can
differ by market, so an item visible in Canada may not be available in the
United States.

We do not currently promote shipping to other countries.

## Shipping availability and cost

Shipping availability, options, currency, and cost are shown at checkout for
the exact items and delivery address in your order. Rates can differ by product,
variant, and destination. A delivery estimate may not always be available.

Do not rely on an old screenshot, cart, or product-page visit as a shipping
quote. The checkout result for the current cart and address applies.

## Delivery estimates

During Puchica's 2026-08-13 supplier-route review, the approved exact variants
showed estimated delivery windows between 7 and 15 days for the supported
Canada and United States routes that were available. Those estimates are a
snapshot, not a guarantee, and can change before an order is placed.

Carrier, customs, weather, address, supplier-processing, and other operational
delays can affect delivery. We will provide the available order and tracking
updates after purchase.

## Where orders ship from

Puchica is a Canadian storefront. Current approved products are fulfilled by
supply partners and may ship from outside Canada, including China. Orders with
more than one item may arrive in separate packages.

## Tracking and order updates

When a tracked service is available, tracking details are sent after the order
has shipped. Tracking can take time to begin showing carrier movement.

If you need help, email **hello@puchica.ca** with your order number.

## Duties and import charges

Customs duties, import taxes, brokerage, or carrier charges may be assessed by
the destination. Puchica does not collect those external charges; when they
apply, they are the customer's responsibility.

## Delayed, missing, damaged, or incorrect orders

Contact **hello@puchica.ca** with your order number and the relevant details. If
an item arrived damaged or incorrect, include clear photos when possible. We
will review the order, carrier, supplier, and policy information before
confirming the available resolution.

Do not mail a return until Puchica confirms eligibility and provides return
instructions and an address. The published refund policy controls return and
refund eligibility.
