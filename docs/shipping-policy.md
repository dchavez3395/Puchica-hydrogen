# Shipping Policy — Puchica

_Paste into Shopify → Settings → Policies → Shipping policy, and/or a
Storefront page. Replace every [bracketed] value with your real supplier's
numbers before publishing._

## Order processing
Orders are processed within **1–3 business days**.

## Delivery times
Puchica partners with a curated network of suppliers to bring you unique
products at fair prices. Because items ship directly from our supply partners,
estimated delivery times are:

- **Canada:** [10–20] business days
- **United States:** [10–20] business days
- **International:** [15–30] business days

These are estimates, not guarantees — customs clearance or carrier delays can
occasionally add a few days.

## Where your order ships from
Puchica is a Canadian business based in Manitoba. Many of our products ship
from partner warehouses located **outside of Canada**, which is why delivery
can take a couple of weeks rather than a couple of days. We tell you this
up front so there are never any surprises.

## Tracking
You'll receive a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships. If you
don't see it, check your spam folder or email us.

## Shipping costs
Calculated automatically at checkout based on your destination.

## Delays, damages, or missing orders
If your order hasn't arrived within **[X] days** past the estimate above, or
arrives damaged, email **hello@puchica.ca** and we'll make it right — a
replacement or a refund.
