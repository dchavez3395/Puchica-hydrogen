## Campaign Structure

### 1. Meta Retargeting ($3-5/day)
**Audience:** Website visitors (30 days), no purchase
**Objective:** Conversions (Purchase)
**Format:** Carousel (4-6 product cards) + Single image story ads

### 2. Meta Prospecting ($2-5/day)
**Audience:** Broad — interests in online shopping, Central American culture, home goods
**Objective:** Conversions (Purchase)
**Format:** Brand story video script + single image ads

### 3. Google Retargeting ($1-2/day)
**Type:** Display remarketing
**Format:** Responsive display ads (auto-sized)

### 4. TikTok ($1-2/day)
**Type:** Spark ads (boost organic-style content)
**Format:** UGC-style product showcase

## Success Metrics (30-day targets)

- **Retargeting ROAS:** > 2x (every $1 spent returns $2+ in revenue)
- **Add-to-cart rate:** > 3% of visitors
- **Purchase conversion rate:** > 1.5% of visitors
- **Cost per purchase:** < $15
- **Email signups:** > 50/month from ads

If retargeting ROAS is < 1x after 7 days, pause and review:
- Is checkout working?
- Are prices competitive?
- Is shipping cost showing clearly?
- Are product images loading?
